# Content and scope standards

Target learners: EMT and Advanced EMT. New clinical content and edits should identify the minimum learner level, sources, and reference-check date. The 10-minute flow uses an explicitly screened bank rather than inferring scope from a topic name.

Scope framework: NASEMSO/NHTSA, *National EMS Scope of Practice Model 2019: Including Change Notices 1.0 and 2.0*, DOT HS 813 151 (August 2021): https://rosap.ntl.bts.gov/view/dot/56917 . Skills tables: document pages 25–32 (PDF pages 39–46). The model guides education; state law, credentialing, and medical direction determine authorization.

AEMT scope examples in the current bank: supraglottic airway placement and peripheral IV initiation. Electrical cardioversion, manual defibrillation, ECG interpretation, and endotracheal intubation are not presented as EMT/AEMT procedures. Recognition, BLS support, and requests for higher-level care may be taught at these levels.

Clinical hierarchy:
- AHA 2025 peer-reviewed BLS/ACLS/PALS guidelines for resuscitation; ALS recommendations must be translated into the learner's authorized actions.
- NAEMT PHTLS and GEMS topic frameworks for trauma and older-adult assessment. Public course pages describe the frameworks, not the full clinical curricula. Do not claim NAEMT endorsement or copy proprietary course questions.
- Accessible peer-reviewed clinical guidelines, systematic reviews, and original studies for specific claims; distinguish their evidence strength from the national consensus scope model.

Short-session references:
- AHA Adult BLS: https://cpr.heart.org/en/resuscitation-science/cpr-and-ecc-guidelines/adult-basic-life-support
- AHA Pediatric BLS: https://cpr.heart.org/en/resuscitation-science/cpr-and-ecc-guidelines/pediatric-basic-life-support
- AHA Pediatric ALS: https://cpr.heart.org/en/resuscitation-science/cpr-and-ecc-guidelines/pediatric-advanced-life-support
- Bulger et al., *An evidence-based prehospital guideline for external hemorrhage control*, Prehospital Emergency Care (2014): https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24641269/
- Alshibani et al., *Under-triage of older trauma patients in prehospital care: a systematic review*, European Geriatric Medicine (2021): https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34110604/
- NAEMT frameworks: https://www.naemt.org/education/trauma-education/phtls and https://www.naemt.org/education/medical-education/gems

Each quick-study item stores its scope link, clinical source, minimum level, and checked date. Short sessions use four screened BLS/pediatric challenge families. The larger legacy question and flashcard banks have not yet received a complete item-by-item scope/source audit and are not automatically included in the new flow.
