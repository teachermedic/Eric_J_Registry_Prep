How this planner works

This original planner combines preparation and active review, inspired by Cornell’s Five-Day Study Plan and retrieval and spaced practice guidance. It adapts to your selected dates and time rather than requiring five days. Review spacing is a practical scheduling rule, not a personalized memory prediction. Completed blocks measure follow-through, not exam readiness.

Saved locally in this browser. Printing uses your browser’s Print / Save as PDF option. Calendar export downloads an .ics file for you to import; it does not connect to or change your calendar automatically.
