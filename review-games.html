Teaching notes & sources

These are intentionally flawed fictional notes. Each case targets one assessment or communication error. The debrief explains the correction; it does not grade your written reasoning. Care follows your training, scope and local protocols.

The national education standards support the assessment framework. AHA and NICE guidelines support the specific breathing and head-injury teaching points; the NICE reference describes a UK clinical pathway rather than a US field protocol. AHRQ supports structured communication.

  • National EMS Education Standards · scene safety, assessment and reassessment
  • AHA 2025 · Adult Basic Life Support
  • NICE NG232 · head injury and anticoagulant history
  • AHRQ TeamSTEPPS · communication and handoffs
